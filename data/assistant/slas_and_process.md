# SLAs & Process

## How to Submit a Request

For any audience request, email **dl-audience@immediate.co.uk** including **"Audience request"** in the subject line. This will populate our board on Monday.com, which ensures we'll get your request done within the lead times outlined below.

In the body of your email, tell us the nature of your request:
- Campaign insight (for audience campaigns)
- Audience insight
- General segment info
- Tracking pixels

Include the brief or as much background information as possible and we will deliver your report following agreed lead times.

## Service-Level Agreements

| Request Type | What You'll Receive | Lead Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Campaign Insights** (for audience campaigns) | A 2-slide document with a summary of behavioural and demographical insights of the users exposed to the campaign + a list of top read articles and article tags, as well as a list of top segments and overlap for engaged users. | 5 working days |
| **Audience Insights** | A 1-slide document with demographical insights for segments created via Audience Project. A 3-slide document with behavioural data for audiences created on Permutive. A 3-slide document with a summary of both for occasions when we have similar cohorts on both platforms. | 3 working days |
| **Audience Size/Reach Estimation** | An email with the figures required. | 1 working day |
| **General Requests** | Depending on the nature, most probably a combination of the above (a PPT with 1 slide for estimated reach and slides for behavioural and/or demographical insight). | 5 working days |
| **Tracking Pixels** | An email with the tracking link for Audience Project (and Permutive in the case of Hubs/Microsites). | 1 working day |
| **Other (Ad-hoc Requests)** | To be agreed via email. | TBC (depending on scale) |

## Tracking Pixel Requirements

We track all audience campaigns by default, **except**:
- Non-standard formats (pre-roll and video formats)
- Client-managed deals (such as PMPs, PDs, and some PG)

To provide insight on campaigns where either of these apply, you **must let us know before the launch date** so tracking pixels can be generated.

Campaign insight reports will not be provided unless requested — drop us an email following the process above.

## Audience Project Surveys

To request a custom Audience Project survey, send requests to **DL-Audience-Ads@immediate.co.uk** with **"Audience request - AP survey"** in the subject line. Questions can be set live within 5 working days, with insights available within one to three weeks depending on response volume.
