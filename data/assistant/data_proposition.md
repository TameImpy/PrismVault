# IM Audiences — Data Proposition

## What is Prism?

Prism is Immediate Media's first-party data commercial proposition. PRISM stands for **P**lanning, **R**esearch, **I**nsight, **S**egmentation, **M**easurement.

## Key Terms

- **Prism** — Immediate's first-party data commercial proposition.
- **Permutive** — A privacy-first audience platform that enables publishers to build and activate first-party data segments in real time, without relying on third-party cookies. It uses edge computing to process data on users' devices, allowing for compliant, scalable targeting and increased addressability across opted-in and anonymous audiences. The Commercial Data Team uses Permutive for behavioural and contextual audience segmentation, with reach numbers for these audiences available within the platform.
- **Audience Project** — A privacy-first audience segmentation and measurement platform that uses declared data from surveys published on Immediate's websites (RadioTimes, GoodFood, etc.) — collected by the Commercial Data Team — to create cookieless audience segments. It combines this first-party data with AI and panel validation to enhance targeting for advertisers and publishers.
- **GAM (Google Ad Manager)** — Reach numbers based on 90-day period for Audience Project segments (Run of Network, UK only).
- **Connect** — Rebranded Salesforce CRM integrated with ad booking tools.
- **Deterministic data** — Data declared directly by users (e.g. via surveys). Also called "declared data."
- **Probabilistic data** — Data inferred by observing user behaviour or modelling from deterministic data. Also called "inferred" or "behavioural data."
- **Lookalike modelling** — The process of identifying users who behave like your target audience by analysing key characteristics and finding similar users.

## Methodologies

Every segment is created in one of two ways:
1. Looking at the content our users visit on our websites (Permutive)
2. Asking our users what we can't guess about them (Audience Project)

### Permutive — Observing User Behaviour

Permutive segments are built based on content users actively browse. We can segment users based on how often and when they have been looking at specific content.

**Example:**
- User A views an Air-fryer recipe on BBC Good Food on day 1
- On days 5 & 6 they view 2 more Air-fryer recipes
- This user then falls into the "Air-Fryer recipes fans" segment
- IM can serve them an Air-fryer ad whenever and wherever they appear across all of our websites

**Data points collected (150+ per page):**
- Article title, URL, tags, type (recipe or how-to article)
- Geographic information (city, country, postal code)
- Technographics (device, internet provider)
- Recipe info (title, ingredients, cooking time, cuisine, course, diet type, nutrition info, keywords, skill level)
- Product info (category, name, price, target age)
- Car info (body style, doors, make, model, fuel type, insurance category, price)

### Audience Project — Asking Users via On-Site Surveys

Audience Project is a platform used to deploy surveys onto our sites. Using survey responses and their panel of 400k users in the UK, we can turn results into insights and targetable segments — allowing us to build audiences where we don't have relevant content or scale.

**Example:**
- User A visits an IM site and receives a survey invitation
- Those who choose "Yes" in response to "Would you ever consider purchasing an Air Fryer?" are collected into one group
- People who look very similar to this group (demographically or behaviourally) are identified
- A new, modelled segment of Air-fryer considerers is created

**How it works step by step:**
1. Agree with IM on the custom audience(s) to create (e.g. supermarket preference)
2. The survey produces rich deterministic data, matched with Audience Project's panel of 400,000 participants and our behavioural data
3. By looking at the behaviour of profiled users, we predict the profile of non-profiled users (probabilistic data), broadening the audience
4. A test set of deterministic data validates and continuously improves accuracy
5. The custom segment can then be used for insights, activation across our sites, or shared with your DSP

**Benefits of Audience Project:**
- Build advertiser-specific segments
- Verify how effectively we've reached the target audience post-campaign
- Provide detailed profiles of a particular target audience
- Minimise use of 3rd party data for audiences we cannot build using our content

### Segment Definitions
- **Fans** — Views related content 2 or more times
- **Enthusiasts** — Views related content 6 or more times
- **Intenders** — Views 4 or more product/car reviews

## Demographic Targeting

Demographic targeting is offered using an onsite survey to ask users directly which demographics they fall into. This enables the most accurate demographic segments based only on deterministic data (declared by users, not inferred).

The survey is applied across all websites, leveraging huge scale and offering highly accurate, significantly sized segments.

**Available demographic targeting options:** Income, Gender, Age, Geographical location, Employment status, Children in household, Household size.

The survey can also build custom segments (e.g. users aged 45+ who regularly shop at Sainsbury's).

Additionally:
- Segments can be shared directly with your DSP or DMP for further insights/activation
- Rich behavioural, demographic, and interest-based insights on who is engaging with your content

## Lookalike Modelling

Lookalike modelling identifies users who behave like your target audience:
1. Analyses the original seed audience to identify key characteristics
2. Finds users who are similar based on those characteristics
3. Broadens your audience while maintaining relevance

## Cookie-less Targeting

Unlike some other publishers, Immediate Media uses a cookie-less data collection and targeting method known as **local storage**. Although similar to 1st party cookies, the main difference is how data is accessed:
- Cookie data is sent back to the server on every web request
- Local storage data is only read client-side (on the user's device) and only makes sense at a per-domain level
- Local storage cannot be used to stitch together a profile of a user across multiple sites (unlike 3rd party cookies)
